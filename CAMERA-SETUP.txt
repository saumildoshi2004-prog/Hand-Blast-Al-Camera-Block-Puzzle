CAMERA UI UPDATE

Desktop/laptop:
- Camera preview is now large and fixed on the RIGHT side.
- Face/hand preview is much clearer.
- Camera is 380x285 on normal desktop and larger on wide screens.
- The game board stays on the left.

Mobile:
- Camera remains large above the board so the hand is easy to see.

For best hand tracking:
- Keep the hand 40–100 cm from the webcam.
- Keep the whole hand inside the camera frame.
- Use a reasonably bright room.
- Avoid a bright window directly behind your hand.
