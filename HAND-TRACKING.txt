HAND TRACKING UPDATE

The new build:
- Uses a lower detection threshold for better hand detection.
- Draws the complete hand skeleton over the camera.
- Shows a large yellow fingertip target on the index finger.
- Changes the target to white while pinching.
- Shows "HAND DETECTED" when a hand is tracked.
- Shows "SHOW YOUR HAND" when no hand is found.
- Uses a slightly larger pinch threshold so picking is easier.

Best conditions:
- Keep the full hand inside the camera.
- Face the palm toward the camera.
- Use good room lighting.
- Keep the hand roughly 40–100 cm from the webcam.
- Avoid strong backlight.
