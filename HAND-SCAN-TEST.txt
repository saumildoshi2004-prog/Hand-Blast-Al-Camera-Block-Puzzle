HAND SCAN DIAGNOSTIC

This build visibly shows whether the camera is scanning and whether a hand
was actually found.

Blue scan box + moving scan line:
  Camera is active and the detector is looking for a hand.

HAND FOUND:
  The hand landmark model has detected a hand. The index finger gets a
  yellow target and the hand skeleton appears.

If the scan keeps saying SCANNING:
  1. Put your entire hand inside the dashed box.
  2. Palm toward camera.
  3. Move closer (about 40–80 cm).
  4. Use bright front lighting.
  5. Remove gloves.
  6. Reload the page.

This build also falls back from GPU to CPU if the browser/GPU cannot run
the MediaPipe hand model.
