REBUILT HAND TRACKING ENGINE

The previous Tasks Vision tracker has been replaced with MediaPipe Hands
direct webcam tracking.

You will see:
SCANNING = camera is running but no hand landmarks found.
HAND FOUND = the model has found the hand.
Yellow index-finger target = landmark 8 is being tracked.

The hand model uses 21 landmarks and feeds the index finger directly into
the game cursor.

Internet is required because MediaPipe Hands and its model are loaded from
jsDelivr.

Run:
START-GAME.bat -> http://localhost:5500
